Everyone who writes, edits or publishes for [hws_site field="name"], staff and contributors alike, follows this code. It draws on the Society of Professional Journalists' Code of Ethics: seek the truth and report it, minimize harm, act independently, and be accountable and transparent.

Seek the truth

  • We verify information before we publish it and say where it comes from.
  • We never invent quotes, people, details or events, and we do not alter photos or video in ways that mislead.
  • We do not present someone else's work as our own. We credit and link to other outlets' reporting when we use it.

Minimize harm

  • We treat the people we cover with respect, and take extra care with children, victims of crime and people in distress.
  • We weigh the public's need to know against the harm a story could cause.

Act independently

  • We do not accept gifts, payment, travel or favors in exchange for coverage.
  • Writers and editors disclose any personal, financial or professional interest in a story, and step away from stories where they have a conflict.
  • Advertisers, sponsors and owners do not decide what we cover or what we say.
  • Sponsored, partner and paid content is always labeled and never presented as independent reporting.

Be accountable

  • We correct our mistakes openly.
  • We explain our choices when readers ask, and we listen to criticism.

Report a concern

If you believe we have broken this code, email [hws_site field="email"]. An editor reviews every report.
