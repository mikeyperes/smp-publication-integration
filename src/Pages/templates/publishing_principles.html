These are the principles behind everything [hws_site field="name"] publishes.

  • Accuracy: we verify facts and say where information comes from.
  • Fairness: we give the people a story is about a chance to respond, and we present different views honestly.
  • Independence: our editors decide what we publish, independently of advertisers, sponsors and owners.
  • Clear labels: news, opinion, sponsored content and press releases are each labeled so readers know what they are reading.
  • Transparency: each story names its writer, and we explain who owns and funds us.
  • Accountability: we correct mistakes openly and answer readers' questions.
