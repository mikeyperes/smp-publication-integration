Every story [hws_site field="name"] publishes goes through an editor before it goes live.

What editors check

  • That the facts are right and their sources are named or linked.
  • That the story is fair to the people in it, who have had a chance to respond.
  • That it is clear, complete and free of plagiarism.
  • That it is labeled correctly: news, opinion, sponsored or press release.

A story that falls short goes back to the writer until it meets the standard.

News and opinion

News stories report what happened and why. Opinion and analysis pieces are labeled as such and show the writer's view. We keep the two apart.

Independence

Our editorial team works separately from advertising, sales and ownership. Sponsored and partner content is labeled and kept apart from our reporting.
