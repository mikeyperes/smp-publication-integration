[hws_site field="name"] aims to get every fact right. When we make a mistake, we fix it promptly and tell readers what changed.

How we correct

  • Errors of fact (a wrong name, number, date, quote or claim) are fixed in the story, and a correction note at the end says what was wrong, what is right and the date it was changed.
  • Significant changes to a story's meaning or headline get an editor's note at the top, so readers who saw the earlier version know.
  • Updates that add new information to a developing story are marked "Updated" with the date.
  • Clarifications are added when a story was accurate but could be misread.
  • Small fixes that do not change the meaning, such as spelling or formatting, are made without a note.

What we do not do

  • We do not quietly change facts without telling readers.
  • We do not remove a published story to hide an error. A story is taken down only for serious legal or safety reasons, and when that happens we say so.

How fast

An editor reviews every report, usually within one business day, and publishes a correction as soon as an error is confirmed.

Report an error

Email [hws_site field="email"] with the story's address, what you believe is wrong and, if you can, a source that shows the correct information.
