When [hws_company field="name"] gets something wrong, we correct it as quickly as we can and say what changed.

How we correct

  • We fix the error in the story.
  • We add a note at the end of the story that says what was wrong, what we changed and when.
  • Small fixes that do not change the meaning, such as spelling, are made without a note.
