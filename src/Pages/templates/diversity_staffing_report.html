[hws_site field="name"] is committed to reviewing the makeup of its staff and regular contributors and sharing what it finds here.

What we review

  • The roles people hold, from contributors to editors and leadership.
  • Broad categories such as gender and background, where people choose to tell us.
  • How this changes over time.

How we protect privacy

Sharing personal information is voluntary. We publish figures only for groups large enough that no one can be identified, and we never publish anyone's individual answers.
