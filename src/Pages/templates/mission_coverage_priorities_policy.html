What we cover

[hws_site field="name"] covers the subjects in its main sections, listed in the site menu. Within them we focus on stories our readers can use: practical guidance, expert insight and the experiences of the people shaping each field.

How we choose stories

  • How much the story matters to our readers and how many it affects.
  • Whether we can add something new: original reporting, expertise or a voice not heard elsewhere.
  • Whether the facts can be checked.

We choose stories on these grounds alone, never because someone advertises with us or pays for coverage.

What we do not cover

We do not publish material we cannot verify, content written to promote a product without a label saying so, or stories that exist only to attract clicks.
