Before [hws_company field="name"] publishes a story, editors check it against the original sources.

What we check

Names, titles, dates, figures, quotes and every factual claim.

How we check it

  • We prefer original documents, recordings, data and named sources.
  • We confirm important facts with more than one independent source.
  • We check images and videos to make sure they show what we say they show.

When we cannot confirm something

A claim we cannot verify is removed, or the story says clearly that it is unconfirmed and who made it.
