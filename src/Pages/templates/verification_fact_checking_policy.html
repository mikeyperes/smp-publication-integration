Before [hws_site field="name"] publishes a story, we check that it is true. Here is how.

How we check

  • Facts and figures are checked against original sources: official records, studies, filings and data, not other outlets' summaries.
  • Quotes are checked against recordings, notes or the person who said them.
  • Claims made by people in a story are checked before we repeat them, or clearly attributed to the person who made them.
  • People and organizations are identified by their correct names and titles, and the people a story is about are given a fair chance to respond.
  • Photos and video are checked for where and when they were taken before we use them.

Showing our work

  • We link to the sources behind our stories wherever we can.
  • We say clearly when something has not been confirmed.

Who checks

The writer checks their own facts first, and an editor reviews every story before it is published, with extra checks for stories that make serious claims about people or organizations.
