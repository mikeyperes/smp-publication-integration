These guidelines describe how [hws_site field="name"] reports, edits and publishes its stories. Everyone who writes or edits for us follows them.

Accuracy

We check facts before publication, rely on named and credible sources, and link to original documents and data wherever we can.

Fairness

We give people and organizations a fair chance to respond to significant claims about them, and we report their response.

Independence

Our editors decide what we cover. Advertisers, sponsors, partners and owners have no say over editorial content.

Labels

Opinion, sponsored content, press releases and contributed pieces are clearly labeled, so readers always know what they are reading.

Sources

We name our sources whenever we can and say how we know what we report.[hws_if page="unnamed_sources_policy"] See our [hws_page_link key="unnamed_sources_policy"].[/hws_if]

Corrections

When we get something wrong we correct it promptly and say what changed.[hws_if page="corrections_policy"] See our [hws_page_link key="corrections_policy"].[/hws_if]
