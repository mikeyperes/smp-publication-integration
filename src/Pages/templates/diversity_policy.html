[hws_site field="name"] wants its coverage to reflect the full range of people its readers are and meet. A wider range of voices makes our reporting more accurate and more useful.

In our coverage

  • We seek out sources of different backgrounds, ages, genders, ethnicities, abilities, beliefs, regions and points of view, not only the most familiar names.
  • We ask on each story who is affected and whether they are heard.
  • We describe people by race, religion, gender, disability or other traits only when it is relevant to the story.
  • We avoid stereotypes in words and images.

In our team

  • We welcome contributors and staff from all backgrounds and judge work on its merit.
  • We look beyond our usual networks when we find new writers and editors.

Holding ourselves to it

We review our coverage and our team regularly to see who is represented and who is missing.[hws_if page="diversity_staffing_report"] Our findings are shared in our [hws_page_link key="diversity_staffing_report"].[/hws_if]
