Readers should know who is responsible for what they read. Nearly every story on [hws_site field="name"] carries the name of the person who wrote it, linked to a profile of their background and other work.

When a story has no individual byline

  • Staff reports written by several people, or short updates compiled by the editors, are credited to "[hws_site field="name"] Staff" or the editorial team.
  • Press releases are published as written by the organization that issued them and are labeled as press releases.
  • Sponsored content is labeled with the sponsor's name.
  • Safety: in rare cases a writer's name is withheld because naming them could put them at risk. An editor approves this and the story says so.

Who is still responsible
